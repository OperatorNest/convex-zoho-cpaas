# 0004: Own channel-aware retries

Enqueue workpool actions with `retry: false`. Completion schedules at most five total attempts with exponential backoff and bounded `Retry-After`. A send or upload failure is retryable only when it may be transient and repeating the identical call cannot create a second provider effect. Zoho has no provider deduplication key for these requests; component idempotency keys deduplicate jobs, not attempts. Only transient HTTP 429 refusals permit retry. Network failures, timeouts, 408, 5xx and every other 4xx are not retryable, including known Zoho codes and daily-limit codes at those statuses. Permanent Zoho codes, account-state failures and retry delays above 24 hours forbid retry; an email 429 daily-limit refusal waits at least 24 hours.

Transport failures without a response record `unknown`. No channel automatically resends an unknown or still-sending outcome. A later attempt that finds a message still `sending` records `unknown` instead of resending it. WhatsApp delivered, read and failed events and email bounce and complaint events resolve `unknown`; WhatsApp transitions stay inactive until live qualification. Email open and click events do not resolve uncertainty, and no event resolves an unknown SMS, so a new SMS send needs a new idempotency key. Completion cannot overwrite a delivery with transport uncertainty. Outcome-less and stuck actions record `unknown` with non-retryable `ZOHO_CPAAS_NETWORK_ERROR`. Suppressions and the effective SMS India endpoint are rechecked transactionally before every attempt.

Caught action failures before `fetch` starts are definitive `ZOHO_CPAAS_INVALID_ARGUMENT` failures; the same idempotency key can create a new send. Failures after the request may have reached Zoho remain uncertain.
